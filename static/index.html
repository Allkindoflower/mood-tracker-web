    How to Use Mood Logger

    • Type how you feel in the input box.
    • Click "Log Mood" to save your mood entry.
    • Your moods appear below with timestamps.
    • To delete an entry, click the ✖ button next to it.
    • Confirm deletion in the popup.

    A prototype app, crafted with care by Uğur Baştuğ

    Note: This is a proof of concept. This app uses simple browser cookies to keep your moods separate. No formal authentication or account system yet.
    Data persistence is limited due to hosting platform constraints (Render free tier). This app is a work in progress; features and stability are not guaranteed.
